ANDREAAAA,

Hallo dengdeng! I know it's been two months since our relationship ended. I've been doing well these past 2 months, or at least that's what I tell myself. Looking back, I think a lot of what I've been doing has just been a way to distract myself from thinking about you. How have you been? I couldn't stop myself thinking about you every day, wondering what you've been up to and how your days have been going. I miss our everyday conversations, especially all those silly little moments we shared. I miss your laugh, your voice, and, of course, *you*. I've come to realize how much I took you for granted. I lied too many times, even about the little things, thinking they wouldn't matter that much. I didn't realize how much those little things could hurt you and damage the trust we had. Looking back, I wish I had been more honest, more understanding, and more willing to make time for you when we still had the chance. If you ever gave me another chance, I'd genuinely want to make things right and show you that I've learned from my mistakes. I know words alone wouldn't be enough to prove that, and I understand that some things can't simply be fixed with an apology. Sometimes, you don't truly realize the value of what you have until it's no longer yours. .

I made this for you as a way of showing my appreciation. I wonder if, by some chance, you'll respond when you receive it, especially since I've been trying to reach you. But honestly, even if you don't reply, knowing that this alone reached you and that you took the time to read these words would already mean a lot to me. I just want you to know that a part of me will always be here, hoping that someday our paths might cross again. I'll keep writing letters for you, even if they're just words that you'll never get to read. Maybe it's my way of expressing all the things I never got to say and all the feelings I still carry. Thank you for being such an amazing person and for being a wonderful partner to me. Thank you for the memories, the laughter, the conversations, and all the little moments that meant more to me than I probably ever showed you. Despite how things ended, I'm genuinely grateful that I got to experience those things with you. More than anything, I want you to be happy, even if that happiness eventually takes you somewhere I can no longer follow. I can't change the past, and I know I can't ask you to come back just because I've finally realized the things I should have done differently. All I can do is acknowledge my mistakes, learn from them, and become a better person. I hope this letter reaches you. I hope life has been kind to you, and I hope you're taking care of yourself. I miss you every day, and I think a part of me always will. Take care of yourself, okay?

thankieeee and love,
